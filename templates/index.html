EMOTION-AWARE ANALYSIS

Understand what people are saying — and how it sounds.

SentioNet analyzes written text and uploaded speech to estimate sentiment or emotion, show confidence, explain the prediction, and provide role-based response guidance. It is useful for customer feedback, support conversations, surveys, learning platforms, call analysis, and human–AI interaction prototypes.
